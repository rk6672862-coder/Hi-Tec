Our Services

1. Partition & Ceiling Removal

Safe dismantling of aluminium, glass, and gypsum board partitions, as well as suspended grid and plaster ceilings.

2. Carpet & Flooring Stripping

Complete removal of office carpets, vinyl sheets, wooden flooring, and tiles to expose and clean the original floor.

3. Electrical, Lighting & AC Removal

Safe disconnection and removal of temporary wiring, network cabling, light fittings, and air conditioning units.

4. Wall Repair & Repainting

Plastering holes, fixing wall damages caused by fittings, and applying fresh paint to meet landlord standards.

5. Scrap & Debris Disposal

Clearing all dismantled materials, scrap, and construction waste, leaving the site clean and ready for inspection.

6. Full Commercial Reinstatement

End-to-end turnkey restoration for offices, retail shops, and warehouses across Colombo.
